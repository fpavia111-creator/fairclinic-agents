# Fairclinic pricing (ver 1)

The software is free (MIT). You pay a fixed price for the setup: we install the agents on your computer, map your exports (Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, Curve or Denticon for dental practices; AVImark, Cornerstone, ezyVet, Covetrus Pulse or Shepherd for veterinary clinics; your 835 remittances, card processor and bank files, payroll register and review exports), set your own thresholds and policies, run the first reports with you and train one person on your team.

Dental files are protected health information. A business associate agreement is signed before we hold any file, and setup is done on your computer.

Prices sit between the restaurant family (Fairtable) and the medical families (Fairclaim, Fairpractice): dental and veterinary back offices are smaller than a medical group's, but dental work involves patient data and insurer remittances. Kurt approved using these proposals.

## Support after setup

- $200 a month for one pack
- $450 a month for a whole practice or hospital
- $250 a month per location for partner rollouts

Support covers changes in your exports (a practice system update that moves columns), new thresholds or policies you ask for, new versions of the agents and a monthly check-in call.

## Included and not included

Included: installation on your computer, mapping your exports, your settings (follow-up days, filing limits, targets, policy amounts), the first run of every report with you, one training session, and a written note of every setting we chose.

Not included: sending anything to an insurer, a pet insurer, a bank, a processor, a lab, a regulator or a client. The agents never bill, file, refund, pay, post or change your practice system; drafts wait for one named person. No clinical, legal, tax or compliance advice, and no determination that a sterilization log or controlled drug log meets any rule.
