# Fairclinic Vaccine Reminders fixtures

Everything here is fictional: the animal hospital, pets, clients, e-mail addresses (example.test) and phone numbers. As of 2026-11-16. Layout: a Cornerstone-style reminder list.

- `reminders.csv`: ten reminders. Planted: a reminder sent on 2 November for a pet marked deceased (Olive); a feline overdue since September with its last reminder 76 days ago, and one overdue since 2 November never reminded (Juniper); an overdue dog with no e-mail or phone on file (Ziggy); rabies due in 9 days with no reminder (Biscuit). Mochi is overdue and was reminded 6 days ago; Pepper is booked; Noodle is not due; Kiwi is inactive.
- `settings.json`: 30 days due soon, 30 days between reminders, 60-day lookback for inactive pets. `settings-typo.json` has a misspelt key.
