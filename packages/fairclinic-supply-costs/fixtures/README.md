# Fairclinic Supply Costs fixtures

Everything here is fictional: the practice, suppliers (Example Dental Supply, Sample Supply Co), items and prices. August to October 2026. No patient data.

- `supply-invoices.csv`: seventeen lines. Planted: gloves up from 8.50 to 9.60 (12.94%) in October; the same composite line twice on invoice ESD-1003; restorative supplies in October at 153.78% of the earlier months; October supplies at 7.62% of production.
- `production-by-month.csv`: production for the three months.
- `settings.json`: 6% target, 10% price increase, 150% spike. `settings-typo.json` has a misspelt key.
