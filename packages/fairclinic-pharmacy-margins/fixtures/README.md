# Fairclinic Pharmacy Margins fixtures

Everything here is fictional: the animal hospital's items, costs, prices and sales. No patient data.

- `pharmacy-prices.csv`: seven items. Planted: a heartworm chewable priced below cost; maropitant's cost up 10.11% with the price unchanged (and its margin under target); gabapentin at a 40% margin; amoxicillin-clavulanate with no dispensing fee. Carprofen, the flea and tick chew (cost and price both up) and prednisone are fine.
- `sales-month.csv`: a month's quantities for four items.
- `settings.json`: 45% target margin, 5% cost rise, prescription items need a fee. `settings-typo.json` has a misspelt key.
